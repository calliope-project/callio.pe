Conversation around Calliope now happens in one place: [our chat on Zulip](https://calliope-modelblocks.zulipchat.com/). Gitter and GitHub Discussions have been retired.

Zulip helps organise conversations. Every message belongs to a named topic inside a channel, so a thread about a solver error or a tricky bit of custom math stays legible weeks later instead of being buried under everything discussed since. A question that takes a few days to resolve can be picked up where it left off.

What this means in practice:

- **Questions, half-formed ideas, and models you are stuck on** go to [Zulip](https://calliope-modelblocks.zulipchat.com/). This is also the place to tell us about something you have built with Calliope.
- **Bug reports and feature requests** stay [issues on GitHub](https://github.com/calliope-project/calliope/issues).
- **Major announcements** — new releases and the like — continue to appear in this [news section](/news/), which has an [RSS feed](/news/index.xml) you can subscribe to.

Whether you have been running Calliope for years or are working through the tutorials for the first time, come and [say hello](https://calliope-modelblocks.zulipchat.com/).
